# Farsofa privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Farsofa is an app for Meta Quest headsets that shows your own game console on a virtual screen.
This policy covers the app itself.

## The short version

Farsofa has no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app is
sent to us unless you choose to send an error report, described below. We do not sell or share
personal data.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your headset, encrypted with a key held by the device:

- Your PlayStation Network account ID and the sign-in tokens issued to the app, so you do not have
  to sign in every time.
- The pairing keys your console gives the app, plus the console's name and network address, so it
  can connect again.
- Your screen settings and presets, your controller options and any button assignments you made.
- A technical diary of your last sessions: what worked, where a connection stopped, how good the
  Wi-Fi was. It stays on the headset unless you send it as an error report.

## What leaves your headset

- Signing in happens on Sony's own sign-in page, shown inside the app. Your password is typed into
  that page and goes to Sony; the app never sees or stores it. Sony's handling of your account is
  covered by Sony's privacy policy.
- While you play, the app talks directly to your console over your network: your controller input
  goes to the console, and its picture and sound come back. This traffic does not pass through us.
- Playing over the internet, away from home: the app asks Sony's PlayStation Network for the
  consoles on your account and for help in reaching yours, using the sign-in you gave it. That
  exchange is with Sony. The picture, sound and controller input then travel directly between the
  headset and your console; none of it passes through us.
- Error reports, only if you send one: when something goes wrong the app offers a "Send an error
  report" button. Tapping "Send" uploads the end of the technical diary to our report server
  (reports.allbele.com, run on Cloudflare). Before it leaves the headset, network addresses,
  hardware identifiers, long identifiers and anything labelled as an account, token or key are
  struck out. A report holds the app version, the headset model and system version, and the
  diary. It does not hold your account, your password or the name of your console. Reports are
  used only to find and fix problems and are deleted automatically after 90 days. To have one
  deleted sooner, write to the address below with the code the app showed you.

## Deleting your data

Choose "Sign out" in the app to erase the account ID and tokens, and "Forget" on a console to erase
its pairing keys. Uninstalling the app erases everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
