# Farsofa privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Farsofa is an app for Meta Quest headsets that shows your own game console on a virtual screen.
This policy covers the app itself.

## The short version

Farsofa has no servers and no accounts of its own. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us. We do
not collect, sell or share personal data, and the app contains no advertising and no analytics.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your headset, encrypted with a key held by the device:

- Your PlayStation Network account ID and the sign-in tokens issued to the app, so you do not have
  to sign in every time.
- The pairing keys your console gives the app, plus the console's name and network address, so it
  can connect again.
- Your screen settings and presets.

## What leaves your headset

- Signing in happens on Sony's own sign-in page, shown inside the app. Your password is typed into
  that page and goes to Sony; the app never sees or stores it. Sony's handling of your account is
  covered by Sony's privacy policy.
- While you play, the app talks directly to your console over your network: your controller input
  goes to the console, and its picture and sound come back. This traffic does not pass through us.

## Deleting your data

Choose "Sign out" in the app to erase the account ID and tokens, and "Forget" on a console to erase
its pairing keys. Uninstalling the app erases everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
